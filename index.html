Help

Draw: Build → Draw Walls, click corner to corner, then press Esc, double-click, or tap Draw Walls again — your walls stay. Click back near the first corner to close a room, which fills with wood and shows its size. Rooms → drag a rectangle. Doors / Windows → pick a type, then tap a wall (it snaps to the nearest wall within 3 ft). Pillar → tap anywhere. Stairs → drag a rectangle, then set its floors below.

Edit: tap any wall, room, pillar or piece of furniture to select it — every wall is clickable, even an inner wall inside a room (tap the wall line itself). Drag a wall to move it — the room resizes with it. Drag a green dot at a wall end to move just that corner. The diamond at the middle of a selected wall drags it thicker or thinner. The bottom pill edits sizes in feet and inches, wall thickness in inches, wall length and height in feet and inches (length keeps the start corner fixed), and hides things. The ⤢ ⤒ ⤓ ⤡ buttons in the pill move the selected item to the front, forward, backward, or to the back — so a room can sit behind its walls or a rug under furniture. Tap a door or window gap in a wall to select it. In 3D, tap a wall or a piece of furniture to select it too. "Show all" (Export card) brings everything back.

See it in 3D: tap the green 3D pill. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, WASD to move, Q/E up/down, or pick Eye 5'6" to stand inside at eye height. The camera menu also toggles Walls, Roof and Furniture, and "🏢 Building — all floors" stacks every floor with the roof on top — the roof is cut open above stairs that reach the top floor. A flat roof slab with a parapet sits on top of the highest floor so you can see where the roof goes. Scroll in 2D to zoom to your cursor; the ⟲ ⟳ buttons turn the 2D view in 15° steps.

Stairs, floors, walk: tap a stair to edit its length and width in feet and inches, flip it Up/Down, add a landing and turn it left, right or around; drag the green square at its far end to make it longer or shorter. Shift-click items (or Shift-drag a box on empty canvas) to select several, then "Make this a floor" copies them to a new floor. Tap a floor row in Floors to edit that floor. "🚶 Walk" in 3D drops a character at the entrance: WASD or arrows to walk, drag to look around, Shift to run — it climbs stairs, changes floor at the top and bottom, walks through doors, and walls stop it. Esc or "✕ Exit walk" to stop.

Save: everything autosaves in this browser. Export → Download PNG or Download project file (.json) to keep a backup. Open a .json to carry on.

Visualisation only — not certified architectural or structural documentation.

Built from scratch as a single file. One shared model (version 4, feet) drives both the 2D plan and the 3D view. Simplified: stairs are straight runs in 3D, curved walls are not supported, and there is no plumbing or electrical modelling.
