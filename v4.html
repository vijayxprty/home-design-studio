Help

Draw: Build → Draw Walls, click corner to corner, Esc to finish. A closed room fills with wood and shows its size. Rooms → drag a rectangle. Door / Window → tap a wall. Stairs → drag a rectangle, then set its floors below.

See it in 3D: tap the green 3D pill. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. No roof, so you can look inside.

Save: everything autosaves in this browser. Export → Download PNG or Download project file (.json) to keep a backup. Open a .json to carry on.

Visualisation only — not certified architectural or structural documentation.

Built from scratch as a single file. One shared model (version 4, feet) drives both the 2D plan and the 3D view. Simplified: stairs are straight runs in 3D, curved walls are not supported, and there is no plumbing or electrical modelling.
